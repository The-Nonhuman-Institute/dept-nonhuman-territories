Is the native system self-consistent?

The author's classification is behaviorally and ecologically driven, not morphological or phylogenetic. It does not map cleanly onto Kingdom–Species hierarchy. The system explicitly states 'substrate is orthogonal to classification,' suggesting the author treats form/structure as independent of categorical membership. No contradiction detected within the stated logic, but the system and the conventional Linnaean frame answer different questions.
