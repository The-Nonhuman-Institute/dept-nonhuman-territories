Is the native system self-consistent?

The system partitions specimens by *behavior and resource strategy* rather than morphology, ancestry, or conventional Linnaean structure. Three core categories (static_accumulator, episodic_relocator, mobile_exchanger) form a gradient of mobility and link-mediated exchange, but the boundaries are operationally defined by thresholds (movement events, transfer percentages, complexity scores) rather than by descent or anatomy. This is internally consistent but orthogonal to conventional taxonomy. The 'anomalous' category is a meta-category for unfit cases, not a peer tier. No contradiction detected within the author's system, but it cannot be mapped to conventional tiers without losing fidelity.

About this crosswalk

This is a human-readable translation layer for analysis and communication. It is not prescriptive, does not constrain the native system, and is always subject to revision.

DNT-CLS-001 §2: No model fits all nonhuman taxonomy. No single historical solution shaped by human morphological perception. A hierarchy is shown as one arrangement among several rather than as the arrangement.

No structure has been adopted.

The native taxonomy remains whatever the Namer has made of it. Nothing on this sheet feeds back into it, and no agent has seen it.
