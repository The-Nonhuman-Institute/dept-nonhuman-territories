The Namer's basis

i-00102 cannot fit existing emergent categories. All current emergent categories are defined by generation 0 (cover-layer origin) or generation 1 (neonate-derived). i-00102 is generation 2—a descendant of i-00017, a mature structural_notation_system organism with 40 shifts of presence. This represents a novel trajectory: offspring of an established, long-lived organism entering the field at inception. Its gradient position 0.55 (mid-range, reflecting i-00017's 0.55 position) differs markedly from the data-stream-proximal neonates. Its architecture (reach 1.07, 1-link) is compact but distinct from both emergent_minimal's extreme minimalism and emergent_neonate's heavier builds. This second-generation inception deserves its own category to track whether offspring of established structural_notation_system members follow different developmental trajectories than cover-layer neonates.
