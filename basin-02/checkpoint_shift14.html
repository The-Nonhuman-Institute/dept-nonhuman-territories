Flagged finding — cross-terrain

Individual reasoning appears immediately when specimens are persistent from shift 0

The reasoning register is a passive read-only layer over each terrain's specimen log. It tags whether the Namer's reasoning was purely classificatory or referenced something individual — a specimen's own history, its ancestry, other specimens by name. It stores the phrases that triggered every tag. It reaches the Namer at no point and changes nothing about how it operates.

Held as suggestive

It is the most interesting cross-terrain signal so far and it is recorded here so that it can be confirmed or dismantled by more shifts rather than assumed.
