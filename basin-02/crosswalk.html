Is the native system self-consistent?

The system partitions specimens by behavioral state (reproductive vs. simple), energy margin (healthy vs. marginal), substrate type (structural vs. fragment), and mobility (stationary vs. roaming), rather than by descent, morphology, or evolutionary history. No internal contradictions detected, but the system is functionally/ecologically organized and orthogonal to Linnaean rank structure. Mean complexity varies widely within categories (e.g., static_accumulator/simple: 8.469 vs. static_accumulator: 1.615), confirming that behavioral/energetic state does not correlate with morphological rank.
