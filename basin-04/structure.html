About these models

These are provisional frameworks for analysis and pattern testing. They do not prescribe ontology. The Namer's system is not assumed to be stable, human-readable, or reducible to any of them.

DNT-CLS-001 Section 1 holds that Linnaean taxonomy is one historical solution shaped by human morphological perception. A hierarchy is therefore shown as one arrangement among several rather than as the arrangement.

NO STRUCTURE HAS BEEN ADOPTED.
The native taxonomy remains whatever the Namer has made of it. Nothing on this sheet feeds back into it, and no agent has seen it.
